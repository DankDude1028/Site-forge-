# Launch shortlist, 2026-10-25

[launch-shortlist-2026-10-25.html](launch-shortlist-2026-10-25.html) ranks 15 single-purpose tool-site
ideas for the first real SiteForge launch. The file is the body of a page without its `<html>`
wrapper: open it in a browser to read it, since GitHub shows only its source.

- **Provenance.** A Claude Code chat session wrote it on 2026-10-25. SiteForge's Research agent did
  not produce it, and it is not stored as a SiteForge evidence packet. Missing evidence packets
  still block site approval in SiteForge itself.
- **Sources.** US Google search volumes and cost per click come from seodata.dev, interest trends
  from Google Trends, and competition from Google result pages. Affiliate terms come from program
  pages. Figures are as retrieved on that date.
- **Scoring.** SiteForge's opportunity weights were applied by hand: demand 25%, low competition 20%,
  commercial intent 25%, original value 20%, low maintenance 10%.
- **Top three:**
  - lawn fertilizer and grass seed calculator, 7.0;
  - party and wedding drinks planner, 6.6;
  - lumber board-foot and cut-list planner, 6.5.
  - A shift-pattern calendar is the alternate, also 6.5.
- **Money is an estimate, not data.** Each year-one figure assumes a share of monthly searches
  becomes visits by month 12, then multiplies by an assumed $5–34 earned per 1,000 visits.
  - lawn: $4–100;
  - drinks: $8–110;
  - lumber: $10–340;
  - about $20–550 for the three together.
- **Decision.** The owner had not chosen a site as of 2026-10-26.
